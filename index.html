Kaboom Kitty

Break up every kitten fight until each kitten has its own cell.

How to play

  1. ›

    Swipe to break up a fight. Push it into an empty cell next to it and it splits in half.

  2. ›

    Big fights split again and again: 4 makes two 2s, and each 2 makes two 1s.

  3. Win when every kitten sleeps alone in its own cell.

  4. ›

    Stuck? Swipe a kitten back onto its twin to rejoin them, or restart.

  5. ✕

    Blocked cells are off limits. No kitten can split into one or sleep there.

  6. ›

    Cracked cells hold a kitten fine, but break for good once it leaves, by rejoining its twin or sliding across.

  7. ✕

    Grumpy kittens from different fights can't sleep side by side.

  8. ›

    Cushions are slippery. A split kitten slides on until a wall, a blocked cell or another kitten stops it.

  9. ›

    Normal: every fight has a fuse. Split it before the fuse burns down, or it blows up.

  10. Big boards: only fights near your last swipe burn. The rest wait.
